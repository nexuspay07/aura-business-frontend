# Aevric AI Private Alpha tester guide

## Before testing

Read the Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, and Private Alpha notice in the product. Use an account created for you or through the approved invitation flow. Do not share access with others.

This is pre-release software. Features may change, fail, or be unavailable. Aevric AI can make mistakes, so verify important information independently.

## Suggested test session

1. Create an account and confirm the legal acknowledgement is clear.
2. Ask a straightforward question and assess whether the response is understandable.
3. Explore an ordinary, non-sensitive personal decision and note whether the reasoning helps.
4. If current information is requested, inspect the displayed sources before relying on it.
5. Save a decision only if you want it preserved, then confirm you can find it again.
6. Try starting a new conversation and moving between the areas available to your account.

## Information safety

Use fictional, sample, public, or ordinary information whenever possible. Do not submit passwords, financial account or payment-card data, government identifiers, health records, confidential employer information, trade secrets, or another person’s sensitive information.

## Helpful feedback

Reply through the channel that delivered your invitation. Include:

- what you were trying to accomplish;
- what you expected;
- what happened instead;
- the page or feature involved;
- reproducible steps and a screenshot, if safe to share;
- whether the issue blocked you or was merely confusing.

Do not include sensitive content, passwords, access tokens, or private conversation text in screenshots or reports.
